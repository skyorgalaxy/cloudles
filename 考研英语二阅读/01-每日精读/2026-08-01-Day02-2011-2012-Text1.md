## 2011 Text 1｜外部董事与公司风险

- [阅读原文与题目](https://english-exam.lazynote.cn/kaoyan/paper/2011-english-two/section2-part-a-1/)
- **文章主线**：外部董事本应独立监督公司；研究发现，他们突然离职常与公司随后表现变差有关。

### 长难句

1. Outside directors are supposed to serve as helpful, yet less biased, advisers on a firm's board.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：外部董事本应在公司董事会中担任有帮助、且偏见较少的顾问。
   - 结构：be supposed to do 表示“理应做”；helpful 与 less biased 并列修饰 advisers。

2. Although a correlation between them leaving and subsequent bad performance at the firm is suggestive, it does not mean that such directors are always jumping off a sinking ship.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：尽管他们的离职与公司随后表现不佳之间的相关性颇具暗示性，但这并不意味着这类董事总是在逃离一艘正在下沉的船。
   - 结构：Although 引导让步状语从句；mean 后接 that 宾语从句；jump off a sinking ship 是“在危机前抽身”的比喻。

3. Firms who want to keep their outside directors through tough times may have to create incentives.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：希望在困难时期留住外部董事的公司，可能必须设置激励措施。
   - 结构：who want... 为定语从句，修饰 Firms；through tough times 表示“经历困难时期”。

### 重点词与短语

| 词/短语 | 文中义 | 记忆搭配 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| outside director | 外部董事；独立董事 | serve on a board |
| compensation committee | 薪酬委员会 | bonus payouts |
| unbiased | 无偏见的；公正的 | unbiased adviser |
| weather a crisis | 安然渡过危机 | weather a crisis |
| proxy statement | 委托书；股东投票文件 | annual proxy statement |
| subsequent | 随后的 | subsequent performance |
| restate earnings | 重述收益；修正财报 | restate earnings |
| class-action lawsuit | 集体诉讼 | federal class-action lawsuit |
| trade up | 转向更优选择 | trade up to a larger firm |
| incentive | 激励措施 | create incentives |

## 2012 Text 1｜家庭作业政策

- [阅读原文与题目](https://english-exam.lazynote.cn/kaoyan/paper/2012-english-two/section2-part-a-1/)
- **文章主线**：作者批评洛杉矶学区限制作业分值的政策；它看似照顾困难学生，实则标准模糊、削弱作业的教育作用。

### 长难句

1. Unfortunately, L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy which mandates that with the exception of some advanced courses, homework may no longer count for more than 10% of a student's academic grade.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：不幸的是，洛杉矶联合学区出台了一项僵化政策：除少数高阶课程外，家庭作业在学生学业成绩中所占比例不得超过 10%。
   - 结构：which 引导定语从句修饰 policy；that 引导 mandates 的宾语从句；with the exception of 表示“除……之外”。

2. But if the district is essentially giving a pass to students who do not do their homework because of complicated family lives, it is going riskily close to the implication that standards need to be lowered for poor children.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：但如果学区实际上是在因家庭生活复杂而不做作业的学生身上“放一马”，它就危险地接近一种暗示：对贫困儿童应降低标准。
   - 结构：if 条件从句；who 引导定语从句修饰 students；that 引导 implication 的同位语从句。

3. Yet rather than empowering teachers to find what works best for their students, the policy imposes a flat, across-the-board rule.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：然而，该政策没有赋予教师寻找最适合自己学生的方法的权力，反而强加了一条一刀切的规定。
   - 结构：rather than 表示“而不是”；what works best... 是 find 的宾语从句；across-the-board 意为“全面一律的”。

| thorny | 棘手的 | thorny questions |
