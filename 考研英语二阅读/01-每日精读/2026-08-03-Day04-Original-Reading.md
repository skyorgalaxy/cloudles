# 每日英语阅读｜Day 04 原创训练

> 目标：完成一篇英语二难度的原创阅读；限时 18 分钟。先做题，再核对答案与长难句。

## 阅读文本

For years, city planners treated libraries mainly as quiet warehouses for books. That description is no longer adequate. In many cities, the most heavily used library spaces are not the silent reading rooms but the areas where students work together, job seekers receive digital assistance, and older residents learn to use online public services. The change does not mean that books have become unimportant. Rather, it shows that access to information now depends as much on guidance and shared space as on the material itself.

This shift has complicated the task of measuring a library's value. Counting loans remains useful, but it tells only part of the story. A visitor who spends two hours preparing for an interview may borrow nothing; another who attends a workshop on evaluating online claims may leave with no printed record at all. If public institutions are judged only by transactions that can be easily counted, they may be encouraged to favor activities with visible outputs over those that slowly build confidence and independence.

The same problem appears in debates about public parks. A small park without expensive equipment may seem less impressive than a large sports center, yet its benches, shade, and open paths can make it the place where neighbors meet most often. Such encounters are difficult to translate into a budget spreadsheet. Still, they can matter when residents decide whether a neighborhood feels safe, welcoming, and worth caring for.

None of this suggests that governments should abandon measurement. Public money requires public explanation. But good measurement begins with a clear account of what an institution is meant to make possible. When the goal is not merely to distribute a service but to strengthen people's ability to act for themselves, the most important results may appear gradually—and may not fit neatly into a monthly report.

## 题目

### 1. According to the first paragraph, modern libraries are changing because they

A. are replacing books with digital services.  
B. are providing support beyond lending materials.  
C. are becoming quieter than they used to be.  
D. are designed mainly for older residents.

### 2. The author uses the examples in paragraph 2 mainly to show that

A. library visitors prefer workshops to books.  
B. digital assistance is difficult to provide.  
C. some important benefits cannot be captured by loan figures.  
D. public institutions should record every visitor's activity.

### 3. The discussion of public parks is intended to

A. question whether cities should build sports centers.  
B. illustrate a similar weakness in output-based evaluation.  
C. compare the safety of different neighborhoods.  
D. explain why small parks cost less to maintain.

### 4. Which of the following would the author most likely agree with?

A. Public institutions should avoid all numerical evaluation.  
B. Visible results are always more reliable than gradual changes.  
C. The purpose of a service should guide the way it is assessed.  
D. Monthly reports are the best way to explain public spending.

## 长难句精析

### 句 1

> If public institutions are judged only by transactions that can be easily counted, they may be encouraged to favor activities with visible outputs over those that slowly build confidence and independence.

- 主干：they may be encouraged to favor A over B。
- if 引导条件从句；that can be easily counted 修饰 transactions。
- 翻译：如果公共机构只按那些易于统计的交易来评判，它们就可能被引导去偏好产出显眼的活动，而忽略那些能逐步培养信心和独立性的活动。
- 关键词：be judged by / visible output / build confidence / independence

### 句 2

> When the goal is not merely to distribute a service but to strengthen people's ability to act for themselves, the most important results may appear gradually—and may not fit neatly into a monthly report.

- 主干：the most important results may appear ... and may not fit ...。
- when 引导时间/条件语境从句；not merely A but B 表示“不只是 A，而是 B”。
- 翻译：当目标不只是分发一项服务，而是增强人们自主行动的能力时，最重要的成果可能会逐渐显现，也未必能整齐地写进月度报告。
- 关键词：distribute a service / act for themselves / gradually / fit into

## 答案与解析

1. **B**。首段强调图书馆增加了协作学习、求职数字协助、公共服务教学等功能。  
2. **C**。两个例子都说明借阅量无法记录部分关键收益。  
3. **B**。公园例子与图书馆相呼应，说明只看可见产出会遗漏公共空间的价值。  
4. **C**。末段明确指出，好的衡量要从机构“要实现什么”出发。
