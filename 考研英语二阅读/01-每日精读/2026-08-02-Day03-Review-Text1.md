# 每日英语阅读｜Day 03 复习

> 目标：不看参考译文，完成 8 句回译；重点检查从句关系、逻辑连接和核心动词。

## 第一组｜从句嵌套

### 1｜2009 Text 1

> But brain researchers have discovered that when we consciously develop new habits, we create parallel paths, and even entirely new brain cells, that can jump our trains of thought onto new, innovative tracks.

- 我的翻译：
- 拆分：主句为 researchers have discovered；that 宾语从句中嵌套 when 时间从句；句末 that 从句修饰 parallel paths 和 brain cells。
- 关键词：consciously develop / parallel paths / trains of thought

<details><summary>参考译文</summary>
但脑科学研究者发现，当我们有意识地培养新习惯时，会形成平行通路，甚至生成新的脑细胞；它们能把思路转到新的、富有创新性的轨道上。
</details>

### 2｜2012 Text 1

> Unfortunately, L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy which mandates that with the exception of some advanced courses, homework may no longer count for more than 10% of a student's academic grade.

- 我的翻译：
- 拆分：which 修饰 policy；that 从句是 mandates 的内容；with the exception of 表示“除……之外”。
- 关键词：inflexible policy / mandate / count for

<details><summary>参考译文</summary>
不幸的是，洛杉矶联合学区出台了一项僵化政策：除少数高阶课程外，家庭作业在学生学业成绩中所占比例不得超过 10%。
</details>

## 第二组｜让步与条件

### 3｜2011 Text 1

> Although a correlation between them leaving and subsequent bad performance at the firm is suggestive, it does not mean that such directors are always jumping off a sinking ship.

- 我的翻译：
- 拆分：Although 表让步；主干为 it does not mean；that 从句作 mean 的宾语。
- 关键词：correlation / subsequent / jump off a sinking ship

<details><summary>参考译文</summary>
尽管他们的离职与公司随后表现不佳之间的相关性颇具暗示性，但这并不意味着这类董事总是在危机前抽身。
</details>

### 4｜2012 Text 1

> But if the district is essentially giving a pass to students who do not do their homework because of complicated family lives, it is going riskily close to the implication that standards need to be lowered for poor children.

- 我的翻译：
- 拆分：if 条件从句；who 修饰 students；that 从句解释 implication 的具体内容。
- 关键词：give a pass to / riskily close to / lower standards

<details><summary>参考译文</summary>
但如果学区实际上是在因家庭生活复杂而不做作业的学生身上“放一马”，它就危险地接近一种暗示：对贫困儿童应降低标准。
</details>

## 第三组｜主语从句与非谓语

### 5｜2010 Text 1

> What makes this slump different from the last, he says, is that there are still buyers in the market.

- 我的翻译：
- 拆分：What makes... 是整体主语；is 后的 that 从句是表语；he says 是插入语。
- 关键词：slump / different from / buyers in the market

<details><summary>参考译文</summary>
他说，使这次萧条不同于上一次的是，市场中仍然有买家。
</details>

### 6｜2009 Text 1

> The current emphasis on standardized testing highlights analysis and procedure, meaning that few of us inherently use our innovative and collaborative modes of thought.

- 我的翻译：
- 拆分：主干为 emphasis highlights A and B；meaning that... 补充说明前句带来的结果。
- 关键词：standardized testing / inherently / modes of thought

<details><summary>参考译文</summary>
当前对标准化测试的重视突出分析与程序能力，这意味着我们中很少有人会天然运用创新和协作式的思维模式。
</details>

## 第四组｜定语与逻辑关系

### 7｜2010 Text 1

> But the market generates interest far beyond its size because it brings together great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy in a way matched by few other industries.

- 我的翻译：
- 拆分：because 引导原因；far beyond 表示“远远超过”；matched by few other industries 后置修饰 way。
- 关键词：generate interest / far beyond / bring together

<details><summary>参考译文</summary>
不过，艺术市场引发的关注远超其规模，因为几乎没有其他行业能像它一样，把巨额财富、强大自我、贪婪、激情和争议汇集在一起。
</details>

### 8｜2011 Text 1

> Firms who want to keep their outside directors through tough times may have to create incentives.

- 我的翻译：
- 拆分：who want... 修饰 Firms；may have to 表示“可能不得不”；through tough times 表示“经历困难时期”。
- 关键词：outside director / tough times / create incentives

<details><summary>参考译文</summary>
希望在困难时期留住外部董事的公司，可能必须设置激励措施。
</details>
