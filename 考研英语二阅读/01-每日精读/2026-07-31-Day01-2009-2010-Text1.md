## 2009 Text 1｜习惯与创新

- [阅读原文与题目](https://english-exam.lazynote.cn/kaoyan/sections/2009/section2-part-a-1/)

### 长难句

1. But brain researchers have discovered that when we consciously develop new habits, we create parallel paths, and even entirely new brain cells, that can jump our trains of thought onto new, innovative tracks.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：但脑科学研究者发现，当我们有意识地培养新习惯时，会形成平行通路，甚至生成新的脑细胞；它们能把思路转到新的、富有创新性的轨道上。
   - 结构：宾语从句 + when 时间状语从句；末尾 that 从句修饰 paths / brain cells。

2. The current emphasis on standardized testing highlights analysis and procedure, meaning that few of us inherently use our innovative and collaborative modes of thought.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：当前对标准化测试的重视突出分析与程序能力，这意味着我们中很少有人会天然运用创新和协作式的思维模式。
   - 结构：主干为 emphasis highlights A and B；meaning that... 补充说明前句结果。

### 重点词与短语

| 词/短语 | 文中义 | 记忆搭配 |

| mindlessly | 不加思索地 | act mindlessly |

## 2010 Text 1｜艺术品市场低迷

- [阅读原文与题目](https://english-exam.lazynote.cn/kaoyan/paper/2010-english-two/section2-part-a-1/)

### 长难句

1. The longest bull run in a century of art-market history ended on a dramatic note with a sale of 56 works by Damien Hirst, Beautiful Inside My Head Forever, at Sotheby's in London on September 15th 2008.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：艺术品市场百年历史中持续时间最长的一轮牛市，于 2008 年 9 月 15 日以戏剧性的方式落幕：达明安·赫斯特的 56 件作品在伦敦苏富比拍卖。
   - 结构：主干为 bull run ended；with a sale... 说明结束时的事件；Damien Hirst 后的书名是作品专场名称。

2. But the market generates interest far beyond its size because it brings together great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy in a way matched by few other industries.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：不过，艺术市场引发的关注远超其规模，因为几乎没有其他行业能像它一样，把巨额财富、强大自我、贪婪、激情和争议汇集在一起。
   - 结构：because 引导原因；far beyond 意为“远远超过”；matched by few other industries 后置修饰 way。

3. What makes this slump different from the last, he says, is that there are still buyers in the market.

   - 我的翻译：
   - 参考：他说，使这次萧条不同于上一次的是，市场中仍然有买家。
   - 结构：What makes... 是主语从句；is 后的 that 从句为表语从句。

### 重点词与短语

| 词/短语 | 文中义 | 记忆搭配 |

| file for bankruptcy | 申请破产 | a bank filed for bankruptcy |
| lose momentum | 失去势头 | growth loses momentum |
| downturn | 低迷；下滑 | economic downturn |
| fluctuant | 波动的 | highly fluctuant prices |
| guarantee | 担保金 | pay out guarantees |
| slump | 暴跌；萧条 | market slump |
| keep away | 不参与；远离 | keep away from galleries |
| lack of | 缺乏 | lack of good work |
